How a month is put together

  1. State your income

    Include bursaries, wages, and family support. Use the amount you can actually spend this month.

  2. List committed costs

    Rent, transport, data, and groceries belong on the ledger before anything optional does.

  3. Assign what remains

    Savings first. What is left is spending you can still choose, or a gap you need to close.
