About

A calmer way through student money.

CoinBliss exists so students can learn the language of money while they are still studying. The work is practical: a monthly budget, a record of expenses, and a short library of terms, reading, and South African student discounts.

What we offer

Fewer late fees, a savings habit, and a clearer picture of what study actually costs.
