Money-saving habits

  • Write the month down before you spend it.
  • Use student discounts where they apply.
  • Cook most meals instead of buying them daily.
  • Choose a student account with fees you understand.
  • Keep a food amount and stop when it is gone.
  • Look for free or low-cost plans first.
  • A part-time shift or a small freelance job changes the income line.
  • Pay on time. Late fees are spending with nothing to show for it.

Glossary

Short definitions for the calculator. For the National Treasury’s own list, see the 2018 Budget Review glossary (opens in a new tab).

Income
Money you can use this month: wages, a bursary stipend, or family support.
Expense
Money leaving the month for a specific cost, such as rent or transport.
Fixed expense
A cost that stays much the same each month, such as rent or a phone contract.
Savings
Money you decide not to spend, set aside before optional purchases.
Left to assign
Income minus expenses minus savings. It is what you have not given a job yet.
Savings rate
Savings divided by income, shown as a percentage. It is not calculated when income is zero.
Late fee
A charge for paying after the due date. It buys nothing.
